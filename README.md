# catching-game

catching-game is a small little game I made for Hackclub Haven. You play as a parrot and you have to move around to collect mangoes and put them in a basket.

Your speed at the start is 400 but you can increase it by 20 for 5 mangoes.

How to play:
1. Use arrow keys to move around.
1. Touch the mangoes to hold them.
1. Touch the basket to put the mangoes in them.
1. The mangoes respawn after 5 seconds.
1. You can only hold 1 mango at a time.

You can play it at https://occult-frog.itch.io/catching-game

No AI was used. Tutorial used: https://www.figma.com/deck/OF7qJyZ1v7CMgvjFWW8vUE/Haven-Godot-Workshop
